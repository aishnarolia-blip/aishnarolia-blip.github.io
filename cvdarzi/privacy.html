CV Darzi

Privacy Policy

Effective 17 October 2026. Published by ShriRakhAish (“we”), developer of CV Darzi: ATS Resume Checker (“the app”). Contact: aish.narolia@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You sign in with Google (or LinkedIn). We use your sign-in only to confirm you are a real user. We do not keep your name, email or photo on our servers.
  • Your CV and the job descriptions you add are checked and scored on your phone.
  • When you ask for an AI interview kit, the app sends that CV and job description to our AI service once to write the questions. Nothing is stored after the answer is returned.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details.
  • You can delete your account and all your data from the app at any time.

Signing in

The app asks you to sign in with your Google account (or LinkedIn account, where offered). From that sign-in the app receives your name, email address and profile photo, and shows them in Settings. Each time the app asks our AI service for an interview kit, it sends a sign-in token so the service can check that the request comes from a signed-in user, and limit misuse. The service checks the token with Google or LinkedIn and does not save your name, email, photo or token. We never post anything for you and never see your Google or LinkedIn password.

Information you give the app

Your CV file or text, job descriptions, the country you choose, your improved lines, your job tracker entries and your interview kits. These are kept in the app's private storage on your device. We do not sell or share them.

AI interview kits

When you open or buy an interview kit, the app sends the CV text and the job description for that application, plus facts the app worked out from them (such as job titles, dates and skills), to our AI service. The service runs on Cloudflare Workers AI and uses an AI model to write the Recruiter and Hiring Manager questions, then returns them to your phone. The CV and job description are used only for that request and are not stored by us afterwards. Cloudflare processes the request as our service provider and does not use it to train AI models. Cloudflare may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and time of the request) for a short time for security and reliability.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Free users see a banner on some screens and can choose to watch a rewarded ad. Google AdMob may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and ad interaction data, to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, and where required elsewhere, we ask for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown; you can change your choice from the app. If you do not consent, only non-personalised ads are shown. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases (Google Play)

Paid reports, kits and passes are bought through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy. We receive a purchase confirmation (product, time, order ID) to unlock what you bought; we do not receive your payment details.

Crash and diagnostic data

Google Play may provide us with anonymous crash reports and aggregated statistics about app performance. These do not include your CV content.

Deleting your account and data

In the app, go to Settings → Delete my account. This signs you out and erases your CV, checks, job tracker and interview kits from your phone. Because we do not keep your data on our servers, nothing else needs to be deleted on our side. You can also remove the app's access from your Google account (myaccount.google.com → Security → Third-party apps) or LinkedIn (Settings → Data privacy → Permitted services). Instructions are also at cv-darzi-ai.aish-narolia.workers.dev/account/delete. Purchases stay in your Google Play account.

Children

The app is meant for job seekers aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

Security and retention

Data you add stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Data sent to our AI service is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and is not kept after the kit is returned. Data sent to Google is kept according to Google's policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, India's DPDP Act, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA or the Australian Privacy Act), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your personal data and to withdraw consent. Because we hold no personal data on our servers, most requests can be met within the app; for anything else, email us and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date.
