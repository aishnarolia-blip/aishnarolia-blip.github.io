Deleting your data

CV Darzi has no accounts and keeps your CV, job descriptions and tracker only on your phone. To delete everything, open the app, go to You → Delete everything, or uninstall the app. Purchase records are held by Google Play.

About the score

The score is CV Darzi's own ATS-readiness measure from a fixed rule set. It is not the score of any employer's or vendor's applicant tracking system, and it does not guarantee an interview.
