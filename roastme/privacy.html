RoastMe AI

Privacy Policy

Effective 22 October 2026. Published by ShriRakhAish (“we”), developer of RoastMe AI (“the app”). Contact: rahul.agarwal0888@gmail.com.

The short version

  • No account and no sign-up. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Roasts are generated on your phone. The text you type and any photo you pick are not uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What the app uses

  • Text you type. Used on your phone to write your roast. It is not sent anywhere and is cleared when you close the app.
  • A photo you choose (optional). If you pick a photo, Android's photo picker gives the app that one photo. It is shown on screen only. It is not uploaded, analysed on a server or saved by the app.
  • Your phone's language. Used to show the app in English or Hindi. It stays on your phone.
  • Free-roast counter. A count of free roasts used, kept on your phone.

Sharing

When you tap Share, your phone's share sheet opens with the roast text. You choose where it goes, and that app's own privacy policy applies from then on.

Purchases

The app does not currently sell anything. If paid features are added, payments will be handled by Google Play, and we will receive only a purchase confirmation, never your card details. This policy will be updated first.

Keeping and deleting your data

We hold no data about you on any server. Everything the app uses stays on your phone. To remove it, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

Children

RoastMe AI is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If the app starts sending data off your phone, for example to an online AI service, we will update this policy and the date above before that version is released.
